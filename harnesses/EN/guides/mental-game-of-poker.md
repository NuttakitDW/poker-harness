## Explained study themes

### Learning model and tilt

Jared Tendler treats emotional reactions as signals of underlying thinking errors, then uses a learning process and written profiles to diagnose recurring tilt, rather than merely telling players to calm down.

Evidence: [PDF p. 66](../../../sources/pdf/The_Mental_Game_of_Poker_%20Jared_Tendler.pdf#page=66) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/mental-game-of-poker/pages-0065-0072.md#pdf-page-66)

### Recognizable tilt forms

The text separates injustice, revenge, entitlement, and other triggers; each needs a different correction because the belief driving it differs.

Evidence: [PDF p. 66](../../../sources/pdf/The_Mental_Game_of_Poker_%20Jared_Tendler.pdf#page=66) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/mental-game-of-poker/pages-0065-0072.md#pdf-page-66)

### Confidence, motivation, focus

Later material connects variance and results to unstable confidence, motivation, and focus. Use session notes to distinguish poor decisions from unlucky outcomes.
