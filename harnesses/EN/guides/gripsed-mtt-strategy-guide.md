## Explained study themes

### Preparation

The pre-game chapter covers study environment, registration choices, and readiness so decisions begin before the first hand.

Evidence: [PDF p. 4](../../../sources/pdf/Gripsed%20MTT%20Strategy%20Guide.pdf#page=4) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/gripsed-mtt-strategy-guide/pages-0001-0008.md#pdf-page-4)

### Early, middle, late stages

The guide changes priorities as blinds grow and effective stacks shrink: early preservation and information gathering, middle-stage steals and pressure, and late-stage survival versus accumulation.

Evidence: [PDF p. 10](../../../sources/pdf/Gripsed%20MTT%20Strategy%20Guide.pdf#page=10) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/gripsed-mtt-strategy-guide/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-10) · [PDF p. 17](../../../sources/pdf/Gripsed%20MTT%20Strategy%20Guide.pdf#page=17) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/gripsed-mtt-strategy-guide/pages-0017-0024.md#pdf-page-17) · [PDF p. 25](../../../sources/pdf/Gripsed%20MTT%20Strategy%20Guide.pdf#page=25) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/gripsed-mtt-strategy-guide/pages-0025-0032.md#pdf-page-25)

### Defense and offense

The final chapter frames initiative, defending blinds, and counterattacks by stack size and opponents, rather than treating aggression as a goal itself.
