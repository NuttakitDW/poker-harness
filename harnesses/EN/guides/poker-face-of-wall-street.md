## Explained study themes

### Poker, risk, and markets

Aaron Brown uses poker as a lens for risk taking and trading: both reward sizing choices under uncertainty, but markets add institutions, leverage, and counterparties beyond a card table.

Evidence: [PDF p. 23](../../../sources/pdf/Aaron%20Brown%20-%20The%20Poker%20Face%20of%20Wall%20Street.pdf#page=23) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/poker-face-of-wall-street/pages-0017-0024.md#pdf-page-23)

### Historical narratives

Chapters follow gamblers, trading pits, crashes, and changing attitudes to speculation. These are historical and financial context, not direct hand charts or current investment instructions.

Evidence: [PDF p. 210](../../../sources/pdf/Aaron%20Brown%20-%20The%20Poker%20Face%20of%20Wall%20Street.pdf#page=210) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/poker-face-of-wall-street/pages-0209-0216.md#pdf-page-210)
