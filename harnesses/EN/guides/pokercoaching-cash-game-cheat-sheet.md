## Explained study themes

### Cash fundamentals

The sheet asks players to value bet when weaker hands call, consider opponent ranges, and adjust preflop play to effective stacks. Its 10:1 implied-odds cue for small pairs and 20:1 for suited connectors are author heuristics, not universal thresholds.

Evidence: [PDF p. 1](../../../sources/pdf/pokercoaching/PokerCoaching_Cash_Game_Cheat_Sheet.pdf#page=1) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pokercoaching-cash-game-cheat-sheet/pages-0001-0005.md#pdf-page-1)

### Opponent adaptation

The sheet separates common player types and asks for responses to their observed tendencies. Use observed ranges and action history before applying any broad label.
