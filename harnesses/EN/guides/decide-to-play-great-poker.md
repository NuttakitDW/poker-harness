## Explained study themes

### Decision framework and position

Annie Duke begins with deliberate decisions, position, raising, and playing ranges rather than becoming attached to one's own cards.

Evidence: [PDF p. 13](../../../sources/pdf/Annie_Duke_Decide_to_Play_Great_Poker.pdf#page=13) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/decide-to-play-great-poker/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-13)

### Bluffs, adjustments, and raises

The middle chapters distinguish bluff frequency and opponent adaptation, then ask how to respond when a prior plan meets a raise.

Evidence: [PDF p. 31](../../../sources/pdf/Annie_Duke_Decide_to_Play_Great_Poker.pdf#page=31) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/decide-to-play-great-poker/pages-0025-0032.md#pdf-page-31)

### Flop texture and hand classes

Separate chapters analyze huge flops, bad position, multiway monsters, textured boards, draws, and top pair on dry versus textured boards. Relative strength matters more than hand name alone.

Evidence: [PDF p. 41](../../../sources/pdf/Annie_Duke_Decide_to_Play_Great_Poker.pdf#page=41) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/decide-to-play-great-poker/pages-0041-0048.md#pdf-page-41)

### River and management

River chapters split in-position from out-of-position play; management closes with the practical context needed to keep making good decisions.

Evidence: [PDF p. 104](../../../sources/pdf/Annie_Duke_Decide_to_Play_Great_Poker.pdf#page=104) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/decide-to-play-great-poker/pages-0097-0104.md#pdf-page-104)
