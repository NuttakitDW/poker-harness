## Explained study themes

### Rules and bankroll

The early chapters teach hold'em streets and initial site/bankroll choices. Site references and income claims are historical; verify current conditions elsewhere.

Evidence: [PDF p. 4](../../../sources/pdf/Cash%20Game%20Killer%20%28Ultimate%20Poker%20eBook%29.pdf#page=4) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/cash-game-killer/pages-0001-0008.md#pdf-page-4)

### Preflop and TAG baseline

Starting hands, position, raises, flop play, and continuation bets form the book's tight-aggressive baseline; ask which worse hands call and which better hands fold.

Evidence: [PDF p. 11](../../../sources/pdf/Cash%20Game%20Killer%20%28Ultimate%20Poker%20eBook%29.pdf#page=11) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/cash-game-killer/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-11)

### Draws and made hands

The draw chapter separates pot odds, implied odds, and reverse implied odds; the next contrasts slowplaying and fast playing across pairs, two pair, straights, flushes, and stronger hands.

Evidence: [PDF p. 19](../../../sources/pdf/Cash%20Game%20Killer%20%28Ultimate%20Poker%20eBook%29.pdf#page=19) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/cash-game-killer/pages-0017-0024.md#pdf-page-19)

### Opponent adaptation and advanced tools

Later chapters distinguish loose-passive players and rocks, then discuss board texture, image, semi-bluffs, a four-step read, floats, scare cards, notes, and table choice.

Evidence: [PDF p. 34](../../../sources/pdf/Cash%20Game%20Killer%20%28Ultimate%20Poker%20eBook%29.pdf#page=34) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/cash-game-killer/pages-0033-0040.md#pdf-page-34)

### Practice aids

The closing section covers patience, tilt, goals, ongoing study, a glossary, odds chart, and starting-hand charts. Charts are aids, not replacements for opponent and stack context.

Evidence: [PDF p. 50](../../../sources/pdf/Cash%20Game%20Killer%20%28Ultimate%20Poker%20eBook%29.pdf#page=50) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/cash-game-killer/pages-0049-0056.md#pdf-page-50)
