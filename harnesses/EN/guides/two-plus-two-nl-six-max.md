## Explained study themes

### Table and preflop positions

Ryan Fee moves from table selection to UTG, middle position, cutoff, button, and blinds. The opening and isolation choices change with opponent looseness and stack depth; position is a central input.

Evidence: [PDF p. 5](../../../sources/pdf/2p2NL6max.pdf#page=5) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/two-plus-two-nl-six-max/pages-0001-0008.md#pdf-page-5)

### Three-bets and squeezes

The guide treats three-betting and squeezing as ways to attack opens and callers, especially in position, while considering who can continue and whether stacks permit later play.

Evidence: [PDF p. 15](../../../sources/pdf/2p2NL6max.pdf#page=15) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/two-plus-two-nl-six-max/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-15)

### Flop decisions

Separate sections cover leading, continuation bets, check-raises, floats, value raises, bluffs, unraised pots, and wet versus dry boards. The reason for betting changes with opponent type and board texture.

Evidence: [PDF p. 17](../../../sources/pdf/2p2NL6max.pdf#page=17) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/two-plus-two-nl-six-max/pages-0017-0024.md#pdf-page-17)

### Turn and river

Turn material distinguishes double barrels, made-hand strength, position, check-raises, and floats. River material treats triple barrels, bluffs, and value raises; review the hand's full line before copying a bet size.

Evidence: [PDF p. 29](../../../sources/pdf/2p2NL6max.pdf#page=29) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/two-plus-two-nl-six-max/pages-0025-0032.md#pdf-page-29)

### Session management

Closing pages address mentality, health, upswings, session length, and multitabling as factors in decision quality.
