## Explained study themes

### Microstakes environment

Nathan Williams focuses on very small online NLHE cash games and player-pool tendencies. The preface stresses that advice from higher limits may not transfer unchanged.

Evidence: [PDF p. 13](../../../sources/pdf/crushing_the_microstakes.pdf#page=13) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/crushing-the-microstakes/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-13)

### Preflop, value, and weak players

The core approach is disciplined starting-hand selection, position, clear value bets against callers, and attention to who actually folds. Judge a bluff against the particular opponent.

Evidence: [PDF p. 65](../../../sources/pdf/crushing_the_microstakes.pdf#page=65) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/crushing-the-microstakes/pages-0065-0072.md#pdf-page-65) · section start unverified; inspect cited page

### HUD, table choice, and review

The book uses online statistics, game selection, and hand review to identify recurring leaks. Historical software or site details should be checked against the current environment.
