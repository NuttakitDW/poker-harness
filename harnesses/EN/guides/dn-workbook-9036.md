## Explained study themes

### MasterClass workbook context

This PDF is a Daniel Negreanu Teaches Poker workbook, with biography, lesson notes, and spaces for reflection; it supports a course rather than replacing all video instruction.

Evidence: [PDF p. 4](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=4) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0001-0008.md#pdf-page-4)

### Position, ranges, and board texture

Chapters 2–4 use position and observed action to narrow plausible ranges. Board texture changes which range has the advantage; the hand review asks for a reasoned estimate rather than a precise read.

Evidence: [PDF p. 11](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=11) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-11)

### Theory, c-bets, and three-bets

Chapters 5–9 connect baseline game theory and pot odds with c-bet plans, check-raises, and three-bet construction. The appendices include example ranges; verify suits visually.

Evidence: [PDF p. 18](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=18) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0017-0024.md#pdf-page-18)

### Bluffing and sizing

Chapters 10–16 cover coherent bluff lines, hand reviews, value/bluff sizing, overbets, multiway dynamics, and mixed actions. A bluff should tell a credible range story; an overbet needs a suitable range advantage.

Evidence: [PDF p. 37](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=37) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0033-0040.md#pdf-page-37)

### Tournament stack stages

Chapters 18–21 distinguish early/middle play, bubble pressure, late/final-table incentives, and fold equity. Reassess the effective stack and payout pressure rather than replaying one default line.

Evidence: [PDF p. 64](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=64) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0057-0064.md#pdf-page-64)

### Cash, tells, table talk, tilt

Chapters 22–29 move to cash-game value and deep stacks, masking/spotting physical tells, table talk, ordered thinking, and tilt management. Tells are deviations from a person's baseline, not universal gestures.

Evidence: [PDF p. 76](../../../sources/pdf/9036_DN_Workbook.pdf#page=76) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/dn-workbook-9036/pages-0073-0080.md#pdf-page-76)
