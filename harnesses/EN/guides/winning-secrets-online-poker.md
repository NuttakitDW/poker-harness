## Explained study themes

### Online setup and game formats

The Fryes cover account setup, online mechanics, game variants, and cash versus tournament structure. Site and security advice dates from 2005 and needs present-day verification.

Evidence: [PDF p. 25](../../../sources/pdf/Winning%20Secrets%20of%20Online%20Poker.pdf#page=25) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/winning-secrets-online-poker/pages-0025-0032.md#pdf-page-25)

### Hand play by street

Chapters on starting hands, flop/fourth street, turn/fifth and sixth streets, and river follow both hold'em and other variants. Keep the street terminology tied to its game.

Evidence: [PDF p. 89](../../../sources/pdf/Winning%20Secrets%20of%20Online%20Poker.pdf#page=89) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/winning-secrets-online-poker/pages-0089-0096.md#pdf-page-89)

### Analysis and discipline

The later chapters use spreadsheets and poker software to evaluate results, then discuss turning data into behavioral discipline and a stable playing environment.

Evidence: [PDF p. 179](../../../sources/pdf/Winning%20Secrets%20of%20Online%20Poker.pdf#page=179) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/winning-secrets-online-poker/pages-0177-0184.md#pdf-page-179) · [PDF p. 211](../../../sources/pdf/Winning%20Secrets%20of%20Online%20Poker.pdf#page=211) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/winning-secrets-online-poker/pages-0209-0216.md#pdf-page-211) · [PDF p. 241](../../../sources/pdf/Winning%20Secrets%20of%20Online%20Poker.pdf#page=241) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/winning-secrets-online-poker/pages-0241-0248.md#pdf-page-241)
