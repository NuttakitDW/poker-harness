## Explained study themes

### Equity and range counting

The workbook begins with equity setups, range building, combinations, and blockers. Write the assumed ranges before calculating; a precise number from the wrong range is unhelpful.

Evidence: [PDF p. 9](../../../sources/pdf/GTO/Poker-Math-Preflop-Workbook.pdf#page=9) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/poker-math-preflop-workbook/pages-0009-0016.md#pdf-page-9)

### Pot odds and EV

Middle exercises cover pot odds, implied odds, breakeven percentages, auto-profit, and EV. Keep immediate odds distinct from money that may be won or lost later.

Evidence: [PDF p. 140](../../../sources/pdf/GTO/Poker-Math-Preflop-Workbook.pdf#page=140) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/poker-math-preflop-workbook/pages-0137-0144.md#pdf-page-140)

### Preflop applications

The final problem sets apply the math to opening, isolation, three-bets, squeezes, four-bets, and preflop all-ins. Challenge mode and major takeaways are review aids.

Evidence: [PDF p. 156](../../../sources/pdf/GTO/Poker-Math-Preflop-Workbook.pdf#page=156) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/poker-math-preflop-workbook/pages-0153-0160.md#pdf-page-156)
