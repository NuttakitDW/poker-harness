## Explained study themes

### History, online play, and specialization

Brunson's story, no-limit history, online play, and the specialization discussion provide context before the variant chapters. Treat online-room promotions as archival.

Evidence: [PDF p. 37](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=37) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0033-0040.md#pdf-page-37) · section start unverified; inspect cited page

### Limit hold'em

The limit chapter requires fixed-bet pot odds, starting-hand selection, and thin value decisions. Its bet structure changes bluff leverage compared with no-limit.

Evidence: [PDF p. 105](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=105) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0105-0112.md#pdf-page-105)

### Omaha eight-or-better

The high-low chapter concerns qualifying lows, split-pot economics, scoop hands, and avoiding quartered outcomes; do not import hold'em hand rankings unchanged.

Evidence: [PDF p. 165](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=165) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0161-0168.md#pdf-page-165)

### Seven-card stud eight-or-better

Todd Brunson's separate stud high-low chapter starts with exposed-card and live-card decisions. Use its stud betting rounds and split-pot rules, rather than Omaha's four-hole-card model.

Evidence: [PDF p. 224](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=224) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0217-0224.md#pdf-page-224)

### Pot-limit Omaha high

PLO high emphasizes starting-hand coordination, nut draws, position, and pot-limit sizing. Draw equity can dominate single made pairs.

Evidence: [PDF p. 265](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=265) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0265-0272.md#pdf-page-265)

### Triple draw and tournaments

The triple-draw chapter is a different lowball game with drawing rounds and hand-selection rules. Tournament overview and no-limit hold'em then return to stack pressure, opponent reading, and aggression.

Evidence: [PDF p. 296](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=296) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0289-0296.md#pdf-page-296)

### Tournament overview and no-limit hold'em

The tournament overview is a distinct section before Brunson's revised no-limit hold'em chapter. The latter discusses starting hands, aggression, and reading people under uncapped betting; it is not interchangeable with Jennifer Harman's limit chapter.

Evidence: [PDF p. 331](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=331) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0329-0336.md#pdf-page-331)

### WPT history and glossary

The World Poker Tour section describes televised poker's growth and tournament setting; the end glossary is a reference for terms, not a new strategy chapter.

Evidence: [PDF p. 415](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%202%20-%20A%20Course%20in%20Power%20Poker%20%28Doyle%20Brunson%29.pdf#page=415) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-2/pages-0409-0416.md#pdf-page-415)
