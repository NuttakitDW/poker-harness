## Explained study themes

### Big-play PLO foundations

Jeff Hwang introduces four-card PLO with the exact-two-hole-card rule, draw quality, nut potential, and the danger of attractive but dominated holdings.

Evidence: [PDF p. 39](../../../sources/pdf/Pot_Limit_Omaha_Jeff_Hwang.pdf#page=39) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pot-limit-omaha-jeff-hwang/pages-0033-0040.md#pdf-page-39)

### Straight draws and preflop

Wraps can have many outs, but redraws and blockers matter. Starting-hand structure, connectedness, suits, and position affect whether a hand can make the nuts.

Evidence: [PDF p. 59](../../../sources/pdf/Pot_Limit_Omaha_Jeff_Hwang.pdf#page=59) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pot-limit-omaha-jeff-hwang/pages-0057-0064.md#pdf-page-59) · [PDF p. 77](../../../sources/pdf/Pot_Limit_Omaha_Jeff_Hwang.pdf#page=77) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pot-limit-omaha-jeff-hwang/pages-0073-0080.md#pdf-page-77)

### Flop decisions and quizzes

After-flop and practice-hand sections distinguish made hands, draws, and pot-size commitments. Use the situation quizzes to test the reasoning, not merely memorize outcomes.

Evidence: [PDF p. 106](../../../sources/pdf/Pot_Limit_Omaha_Jeff_Hwang.pdf#page=106) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pot-limit-omaha-jeff-hwang/pages-0105-0112.md#pdf-page-106) · [PDF p. 125](../../../sources/pdf/Pot_Limit_Omaha_Jeff_Hwang.pdf#page=125) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/pot-limit-omaha-jeff-hwang/pages-0121-0128.md#pdf-page-125)

### Omaha eight-or-better

The latter large section switches to limit and pot-limit Omaha eight-or-better. High and low halves, qualifying lows, counterfeit risk, and scoop potential require a different model from high-only PLO.
