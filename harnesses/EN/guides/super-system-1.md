## Explained study themes

### General poker strategy

Doyle Brunson frames poker as observation of people, with sections on tells, superstition, ethics, competitiveness, bankroll, courage, breaks, versatility, and tournaments.

Evidence: [PDF p. 2](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%201.pdf#page=2) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-1/pages-0001-0008.md#pdf-page-2)

### Limit and no-limit

A bridge chapter compares betting structures. A tactic that is viable with capped raises need not carry over unchanged to uncapped no-limit bets.

Evidence: [PDF p. 28](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%201.pdf#page=28) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-1/pages-0025-0032.md#pdf-page-28) · section start unverified; inspect cited page

### No-limit hand classes

The no-limit chapter treats antes, AA/KK, AK, queens, smaller pairs, connectors, marginal hands, trash, short-handed play, and insurance. It repeatedly connects preflop hand value to position, opponent, and postflop leverage.

Evidence: [PDF p. 42](../../../sources/pdf/Doyle%20Brunson_s%20Super%20System%201.pdf#page=42) · [extracted page](../../EN/sources/super-system-1/pages-0041-0048.md#pdf-page-42)
