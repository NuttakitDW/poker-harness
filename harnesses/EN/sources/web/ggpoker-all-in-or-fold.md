# All-in or Fold table information (GGPoker)

Source: GGPoker, All-In or Fold game page, table information
URL: https://ggpoker.com/poker-games/all-in-or-fold/
Checked: 2026-09-26
Evidence: Official table of stakes, buy-ins, rake, jackpot fee and All-In Fortune fee for All-in or Fold Hold'em and Omaha; checked against a hand history from AFHBlue7, $0.05/$0.10 4-max, 2026-09-26.

- Every hand is all-in or fold. Available for Hold'em and Omaha.
- Hold'em low stakes: $0.05/$0.10, buy-in $1, rake 0.06 BB, jackpot fee 0.07 BB, All-In Fortune fee $0.007, jackpot payout 0.01%. $0.10/$0.25, buy-in $2, rake 0.04 BB, jackpot fee 0.04 BB, Fortune $0.01. $0.25/$0.50, buy-in $4, rake 0.04 BB, jackpot fee 0.06 BB, Fortune $0.02.
- Hold'em middle and high stakes ($0.50/$1 up to $50/$100): rake 0.05 BB, jackpot fee 0.05 BB; buy-in 8 BB (for example $8 at $0.50/$1, $80 at $5/$10).
- Omaha: $0.10/$0.20 buy-in $1, rake 0.03 BB, jackpot fee 0.035 BB; most other Omaha stakes rake 0.03 BB or 0.025 BB.
- Play-money AoF Hold'em lists rake as 0.06BB per hand with no cap, so the rake is a fixed amount per hand, not a share of the pot.
- 50% of the fee for all AoF games goes to the jackpot. The AoF Hold'em jackpot needs a straight flush or better using both hole cards, and the hand must reach showdown.
- The page says "Try your luck with the All-In or Fold Jackpot and All-In Fortune whenever you go All-In".
- The page does not say which hands the fee is charged on. Third-party guides say folds and shoves that win the blinds pay nothing.
- Hand history check, 5 hands at $0.05/$0.10: no rake, jackpot or fee lines, and the winner always collected the whole pot (2.10 = 1 + 1 + 0.10 blind). The fee is taken outside the pot and does not appear in the hand history.
