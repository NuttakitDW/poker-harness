# Poker rankings and results

- Stable ID: `24-poker-rankings-and-results`
- Scope: Interpreting dated rankings, tournament cashes and year-based points.
- Thai counterpart: [อันดับและผลงานโป๊กเกอร์](../../TH/topics/24-poker-rankings-and-results.md)

## Core idea

“Number one” needs a metric, population and date. The Hendon Mob's Thailand country list generated 20 September 2026 ranked recorded live tournament cashes: Punnat Punsri first ($39,220,401), Kannapong Thanarattrakul second ($4,156,995), and Phachara Wongwichit third ($2,998,125). The list's country category is not proof of legal nationality; the table also excludes recurring daily, weekly and monthly results. Gross cashes do not subtract buy-ins, travel or other costs and do not measure net profit or playing skill.

GPI (Global Poker Index) instead ranks qualifying live tournament finishes weekly using a rolling preceding 36 months, including field/finish position, buy-in and recency. Its Player of the Year race uses a single calendar year. A dated historical snapshot must never be presented as today's leader. If no verified fresh data exists, say when the evidence was generated and identify the metric.

## Worked example (authored; not quoted from source)

Authored example: “On Hendon Mob's Thailand live-tournament money list generated 20 September 2026, Punnat Punsri was first by recorded gross cashes. That does not establish who was the most skilled player.”
