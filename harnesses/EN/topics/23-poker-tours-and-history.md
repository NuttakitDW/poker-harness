## Core idea

WSOP began at Binion's in 1970, and its Main Event became a widely recognized tournament title. WPT was founded by Steven Lipscomb in 2002 as a televised international tour. EPT (European Poker Tour) began in Barcelona in September 2004; Alexander Stevic won its first event. APT (Asian Poker Tour) is an Asia-Pacific tournament circuit; use a separately verified source for founding chronology. Triton Poker Series runs international high-stakes events and broadcasts; its 2019 Triton Million for Charity was won by Aaron Zang, and Triton ONE launched in Jeju in 2025.
