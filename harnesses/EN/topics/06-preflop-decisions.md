# Preflop decision sequence

- Stable ID: `06-preflop-decisions`
- Scope: No-limit hold’em; charts are starting hypotheses, not commands.
- Thai counterpart: [ลำดับคิดก่อน flop](../../TH/topics/06-preflop-decisions.md)

## Core idea

Before entering, identify format, positions, effective stacks, open size, rake or ante, and players yet to act. Choose among fold, call and raise by comparing the range you represent with likely responses. A raise can win blinds, isolate weaker ranges, or build a pot with an equity edge; a call preserves weaker hands but may invite multiway play and deny initiative. Re-evaluate after a three-bet or squeeze rather than defending the original opening range wholesale. Record why marginal hands profit in this exact seat and stack depth.

## One decision tree

- **Unopened pot:** identify how many players remain and whether their blind defense is tight or loose. Build a candidate raise range; compare fold and call where allowed.
- **Facing an open:** distinguish value three-bets, hands playable as calls and hands too weak given position and rake. Do not call merely because a hand appears in an opening range.
- **Facing a three-bet:** recompute effective stack, pot odds and opponent range. A strong-looking broadway hand can be dominated against a tight three-bet range; small pairs need enough future payoff to justify set mining.
- **Short stack:** a small raise may commit much of your stack and invite awkward calls. Compare raise/fold, raise/call and shove under the actual tournament or cash rules.

## Worked example (authored; not quoted from source)

Authored example: A small pocket pair with 20 BB effective cannot assume the same set-mining payoff as with 150 BB; future winnings are capped by the shorter stack.

## Practice and boundary checks

Rake and antes pull preflop incentives in different directions: rake can punish small marginal cash-game pots, while antes increase the dead money available to win in tournaments. Position also changes realization: the same suited connector may be easier to play in position than out of position even if its raw equity against an opening range is identical. When choosing a three-bet size, consider whether callers receive attractive pot odds and how much stack remains. Record the full preflop line because “I had AQ” is not enough to evaluate a fold, call or four-bet.

## Common errors

Copying a deep-stack opening chart into short-stack or high-rake games.
