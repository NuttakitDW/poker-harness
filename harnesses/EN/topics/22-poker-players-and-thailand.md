# Poker players and Thailand

- Stable ID: `22-poker-players-and-thailand`
- Scope: Recognizing players and answering Thai poker people questions with dated evidence.
- Thai counterpart: [นักโป๊กเกอร์และผู้เล่นไทย](../../TH/topics/22-poker-players-and-thailand.md)

## Core idea

Poker people are a legitimate topic alongside strategy. Punnat Punsri (ปุณณัตถ์ ปุณศรี) has documented wins at the 2023 APT Taipei Main Event, the 2022 Triton Mediterranean Poker Party $100k event, and the 2023 PokerGO Cup $25k event. The APT's 2023 report called him Thailand's all-time live tournament money-list leader then; GPI named him its 2025 Player of the Year. Other recorded Thai-country-list names include Kannapong Thanarattrakul, Phachara Wongwichit, Pakinai Lisawad and Phanlert Sukonthachartnant; Natural8 has used “Kannapong Tent” as Kannapong's online name. Chris Moneymaker won the 2003 WSOP Main Event; Daniel Negreanu and Phil Ivey were inducted into the Poker Hall of Fame in 2014 and 2017 respectively. Doyle Brunson won WSOP Main Events in 1976 and 1977 and published Super/System in 1978; Kristen Foxen won GPI's 2025 Female Player of the Year. These are dated achievements, not a ranking of ability.

An unclear spoken name is not proof of identity or an alias. Ask for the person's name again, or offer a tentative match if the surrounding clue supports it. Never declare a garbled name to be Punnat's nickname.

## Worked example (authored; not quoted from source)

If asked whether an unclear name is “the number-one Thai poker player,” answer: “Do you mean Punnat Punsri? Hendon Mob listed him first on its Thailand live-tournament cashes list generated 20 September 2026, but I want to check the name you said.” This is an authored response pattern, not a quote or proof that the unclear sound refers to him.
