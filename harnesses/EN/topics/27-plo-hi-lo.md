# PLO hi/lo split

- Stable ID: `27-plo-hi-lo`
- Scope: Pot-Limit Omaha hi/lo eight-or-better hand selection, position, scooping, quartering and freerolls, from Jeff Hwang's Chapter 9 principles.
- Thai counterpart: [PLO Hi/Lo แบ่ง pot สูงต่ำ](../../TH/topics/27-plo-hi-lo.md)

## Core idea

PLO hi/lo is not a sharing game: play to scoop, not for half. Getting quartered in pot-limit is a real loss because most money goes in after the flop. Nearly every hand played should hold a suited ace; the best hands win both ways (A23K wheel-wraps, A2KQ Broadway-wraps with a wheel card, AA with low potential). Versus limit, AA and big pairs with two small cards (KK43) gain value while high-only hands and middle wraps lose it. Position is king: in position a bare nut low becomes a weapon; out of position play premiums and rarely raise up front. Facing action, draw only to the nuts and bet the full pot. Top set or a naked straight on a low two-flush board is often freerolled — deep-stacked, check and fold.

## Worked example (authored; not quoted from source)

A2KQ with a suited ace is a strong two-way hand: nut low draw plus Broadway and the nut flush. A naked straight on a low two-tone board facing a raise deep-stacked is often a release.
