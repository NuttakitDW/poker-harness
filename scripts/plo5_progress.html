How sessions are compared

  • Every session is scored with the same rules as its review page: only trusted ratings count, and a decision is a mistake only when you took an action the solver uses under 10% of the time and it cost more than the sampling noise.
  • Equity is in buy-ins so sessions with different prize pools compare fairly, and per 100 decisions so long and short sessions compare fairly.
  • "Matched the solver" counts decisions where your action is one the solver takes at least 25% of the time.
  • A single session is a small sample (a few dozen trusted decisions). Read the trend over several sessions, not one jump.
